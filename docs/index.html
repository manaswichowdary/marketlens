Trend over time

There is 1 day of daily snapshots so far. A trend needs at least 14 days, measured the same way every day, so this page does not draw one yet. It will appear on its own once the daily runs have built up enough history.

What this does not show

  • It covers jobs on about 36 company job boards plus one remote jobs feed. It is not the whole market, and it leans toward tech companies.
  • A share is how often a skill is named in a posting, not how much employers need it.
  • Skills come from a fixed dictionary, so a skill not in the dictionary is never counted.
  • Only jobs that are still open are seen. Filled jobs disappear before they can be counted.
